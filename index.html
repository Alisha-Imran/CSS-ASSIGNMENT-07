FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services and booking process.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book online using the appointment form above, call our clinic directly at +1 (800) CARDIO, or email us. We confirm all appointments within 2 business hours.

What heart conditions do you treat?

We treat a comprehensive range of cardiovascular conditions including coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, hypertension, valvular heart disease, peripheral artery disease, and more.

Do you offer emergency consultations?

Yes, our emergency cardiac care line is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

What insurance plans are accepted?

We accept most major insurance plans. Please contact our billing team to verify your specific plan coverage before your appointment.

How long does a first consultation take?

An initial consultation typically takes 45-60 minutes, including a medical history review and physical examination.

Do you offer telemedicine consultations?

Yes, we offer secure video consultations for follow-up visits, medication reviews, and initial assessments.
